## 1. What was built since Nov 24

**Step 3a: Business Search and Pass 1** (menu: Business Search)
- Pick a business type, Long Island towns or zips ("All of Nassau" / "All of Suffolk"), and the list (Get Seen / Ordering). No 3-zip limit. The credit estimate is shown and confirmed before anything runs.
- Google Maps search (SerpAPI, 30-day cache, monthly budget with a hard stop). Duplicates removed by phone, website, and name + address.
- Website check (own site only): loads, copyright year, phone-friendly, contact form, site builder, ordering company.
- Social links from the website's raw page source. If Facebook or Instagram is missing, **one combined Facebook + Instagram search** (the spec says one search each; combined to save credits).
- Ordering list: delivery apps by search, plus **6b, built**: delivery and order buttons on their own site, with the page each is on. "Sends customers to delivery apps from own site" is Yes/No, or **Unknown** for sites that load buttons with JavaScript. Own ordering and platform, and Google's delivery/takeout options.
- Still-open check: Google's closed status, Google AI summary (labeled as a hint), and old sources (only from the name search, because old Facebook posts gave false alarms).
- Lead types, results table, filters, CSV and Excel, hand-check fields, and Approve into Contacts and Pipeline (contact and deal created with the findings in the notes).
- **Added (Seann's request): skip businesses already found** in earlier searches, at 0 credits. The estimate subtracts them. Pick a higher "How many per place" to find new ones in the same place.
- **Check again** button: redoes the checks for a search without a new Maps search (saved searches are reused free).

**Step 3b: Opportunity score**
- Points for what TownGrid can fix or sell, plus "alive" points, with the reasons shown on each business (e.g. "+25 no website · +10 rating 4.6 / 157 reviews").
- Every value is editable in Settings → Opportunity score. Saving re-scores everything for free.
- Knock-outs are set aside, not scored: Google closed, same name at 2+ addresses (chain), or set aside by hand with a reason.

**Emails (Seann's request: every legal way, cheapest first, stop when found)**
1. The business's own website: mail icons and links, written emails, emails hidden by Cloudflare. The Contact page is read first. Free.
2. Emails in the search results we already have, e.g. a Facebook page's contact line in Google's snippet. Facebook is never opened. Free.
3. One Google "name + town + email" search. 1 SerpAPI credit.
4. AI web search (Claude Opus 5.5, search only, never opens pages). An email is kept **only when a source it cites shows that exact email**. Capped at $20 a month.
5. Seann's own entry ("Email you found" in the hand check). It always wins.
- Emails from search results or AI must belong to the business: their own web address, a domain with their name, or a gmail-type address containing part of their name. (A snippet of La Bussola's Instagram had shown the Chamber of Commerce's email.)
- Steps 3–4 run from "Find missing emails" (tiers A and B by default) or "Find email" on one business, with the cost shown first. A step is never paid for twice for the same business.

**Step 3c: Google profile check (Pass 2)**, for businesses scoring 40+ before it
- Unclaimed listing, owner replies among the newest 10 reviews, unanswered 1–2 star reviews, newest review date, "Updates" posts in the last 90 days, photos from the owner.
- About **3 credits a business, not 4**: the posts list is only fetched when the place details don't show dated posts.

## 3. Changes from the spec (decided while building)
- **Facebook + Instagram:** one combined search instead of one each.
- **Score, "no website":** +25 only. The +20 "Website button" line applies when the button goes to Facebook, is a dead link, or is missing while a website exists elsewhere (Seann, Nov 24).
- **Score, restaurants:** they get the Get Seen points too, on top of the ordering points (Seann, Nov 24).
- **Score, "website owned by the ordering company":** only real websites by Slice, ChowNow, Owner.com, Menufy or BeyondMenu. A Toast order page used as the website counts as "no website", and website builders like BentoBox don't count.
- **Score, "no owner photo in 6 months":** Google gives no photo dates, so this means **"no photos from the owner at all"**.
- **Tier lines:** **A from 85, B from 60** (was 60 / 40). Pass 2 can add up to 70 points, which made 9 of 10 tier A.
- **Google profile check:** has its **own "from 40" line**, separate from tier B, so businesses at 40–59 before the check still get checked. The Metropolitan went from 45 to 100.
- **Pass 2 cost:** about 3 credits a business, not 4.
- **Results sort:** by score (best first), then lead type and review count. The spec said lead type, then reviews.
- **SerpAPI:** Starter plan (1,000 a month). The CRM stops at 900 and runs at most 100 an hour.
- **Anthropic:** TownGrid's own Anthropic organization, one key per service. The CRM's key is set directly on the crm service, not through a shared variable.
