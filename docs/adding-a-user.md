# Adding someone to TownGrid CRM

Replaces the old ESyCRM onboarding guide (removed Nov 22, 2026).

There are no passwords. People sign in with a 6-digit code emailed to them, and only
addresses already in the CRM can sign in.

1. Sign in as an admin and open **Users** (left menu, under Admin).
2. Click **Add User**, type their email address and name, and pick the role:
   - **Salesperson** sees only their own contacts, deals and tasks.
   - **Admin** sees everything and can change settings, plans and users.
3. Send them the CRM address. They type their email, click **Email me a code**, and type the code.

To remove access, delete the user in **Users**. They are signed out on their next click.

Commissions and Payroll are hidden from the menu until TownGrid has salespeople and
commission rules (Seann decides the rules; every plan pays 0 until then).
